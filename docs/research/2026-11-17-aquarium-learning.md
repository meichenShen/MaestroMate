# Aquarium: editorial basis for beginner activities
Checked2026-11-17. Keep historical facts, listening interpretation and original practice distinct.

Aquarium is No.7 of fourteen movements in The Carnival of the Animals, composed1886. The suite's largely private, playful beginnings complement Saint-Saëns's public career. Do not attribute our invented scenes to him. Philharmonie de Paris: https://pad.philharmoniedeparis.fr/0775652-le-carnaval-des-animaux-saint-saens.aspx?_lg=fr-FR ; biography: https://www.operadeparis.fr/en/artists/camille-saint-saens . Exact first-performance day varies between consulted notes and is omitted.

Score catalogue: https://imslp.org/wiki/Le_Carnaval_des_Animaux_(Saint-Sa%C3%ABns,_Camille) . Do not label the whole suite's instrumentation as every instrument playing in Aquarium. Flowing piano figures and sustained flute/string lines support a melody/accompaniment invitation. Original glass-harmonica colour is often substituted in performance. The learning example ABA is not a claimed formal analysis or transcription of Aquarium.

Critical interpretation: https://www.laphil.com/works/the-carnival-of-the-animals describes flowing, shimmering textures and an underwater atmosphere. Copy paraphrases that idea, welcomes alternative imagery and uses no critic quotation.

Performance: timbre, tempo, balance and space depend on interpretation. Kevin MacLeod's produced recording has its own full attribution in web/public/audio/ATTRIBUTION.md. Clips0–19,68–88,109–127 seconds refer only to that fixed full mix, never isolated original instruments. No performer interview, stem separation or recording comparison is claimed.

Teaching: pitch example C-D-E-G-A distinguishes high/low from loud/quiet. Return example C-E/A-G/C-E uses three visual phrases, then explains matching note order. Variation copies C-D-E-G but changes G to C,D or E; all three endings are accepted. Evaluation checks a stated constraint, never musical taste. Pool transfers it into eight editable spaces. Future rhythm/harmony work should follow trial feedback.

Trial with interested children/classmates: observe independent start/replay, explanation of pitch, recognition of return, intentional change and navigation. Ask what was tricky. The optional feedback prompt asks for no names/schools; email remains optional. No new analytics, identity records, recordings or chat transcripts are collected.
