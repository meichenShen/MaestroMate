Original visual assets
The theatre, underwater environment, swan, composer sculpture and detective icon
are generated pre-rendered illustrations with realistic 3D art direction. They are
images, not live 3D models. The composer sculpture is an imagined portrait.
The aquarium poster is a frame captured from MaestroMate's live 3D scene.
